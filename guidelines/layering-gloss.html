The bug this rule exists for
Claim!
An opaque sheen at Shell+1 with labels left at their default index paints a solid white lozenge straight over every caption. Under ZIndexBehavior.Sibling this hit ~30 call sites at once and made the whole HUD unreadable.

Never hand-roll a sheen — build surfaces through UITheme, which owns both halves of the contract.
